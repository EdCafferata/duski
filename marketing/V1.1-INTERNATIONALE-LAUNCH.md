## 1. Press release (EN) — v1.1

**Headline:** Duski, the sleep-sound app that generates every sound on-device, now speaks eight languages

**Sub:** Version 1.1 for iPhone adds English, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian and Japanese — and a yearly plan.

**[CITY], [DATE]** — The IT Crowd today released Duski 1.1 for iPhone. Duski is a sleep-sound mixer that
generates all of its audio on the device itself: users layer brown noise, rain, a heartbeat or womb sounds
into their own mix, each layer with its own volume. Nothing is streamed, no account is needed and the app
contains no advertising.

Until now Duski was available in Dutch only. Version 1.1 adds seven languages and a yearly Premium plan
next to the existing monthly one.

When the app is set up for a baby, it adjusts its content and caps the volume. "Standalone white-noise
machines are often louder than is good for an infant when turned all the way up," says maker Ed Cafferata.
"We wanted the app itself to take care of that, instead of leaving it to a tired parent at 3 a.m."

**Facts**
- 26 sounds in four groups: noise colours, nature, body & baby, and other (including our own arrangements
  of public-domain pieces by Bach, Pachelbel, Satie and Debussy)
- Sleep timer from 15 to 90 minutes with fade-out
- Five age groups: baby, child, teen, adult, senior
- Free to download with four free sounds. Premium unlocks everything: first month free, then $0.99 per
  month or $8.99 per year (€0.99 / €9.99), cancel anytime
- iPhone, App Store: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6806373286

**Contact:** Ed Cafferata — the-it-crowd@cafferata.info · Press images: on request

_Nog door Ed te bevestigen: plaatsnaam, het citaat (de zin over "3 a.m." is nieuw), en of de Android-versie genoemd mag worden._
